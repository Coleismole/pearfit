Privacy notice

Last updated 12 November 2026

This notice says what Pearfit collects when you visit this site, use the live size demo or the shopper app, or send us an inquiry, who handles it for us, and what you can choose. Questions or requests: customerservice@pearfit.app.

In short

  • Analytics (PostHog) and page speed measurement (Vercel Speed Insights) load only if you accept in the banner. With no answer, or a decline, they do not load.
  • Measurements you type into the demo or the app go to our size service to work out an answer. The service does not store them. The app keeps your wardrobe in your own browser.
  • The site serves its own fonts and loads nothing from ad networks or font services.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

What we collect and why

Visiting the site

Our host, Vercel, processes each request to send you a page and to keep the site secure and running. That includes your IP address, the page you asked for, and your browser and device type. We have a legitimate interest in running a secure site.

Analytics, only if you accept

If you accept, the site loads PostHog (hosted in its US region) to understand how the site is used. PostHog may receive the pages you view, which sections you scroll to, the links you click, how you use the demo, the stores search and the calculators, your browser and device type, an approximate location worked out from your IP address, and an identifier kept in your browser. It may also record a replay of your visit: what is on screen, clicks, scrolling and page changes. It also receives errors raised in your browser.

What the demo, the stores search and the calculators send is limited to counts, options from our own lists, our own product ids and, if you paste a product link or type a domain name, that store’s hostname. The shirts and measurements you type, the rest of what you type into a search or a link, review text, names and email addresses are not sent. The demo form and the inquiry form are excluded from replays. Our basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time (see “Your choices”).

Page speed, only if you accept

If you accept, the site also loads Vercel Speed Insights, which measures how fast pages load for visitors.

The inquiry form

If you send the brand pilot form, we receive your name, work email, storefront URL and monthly order volume. The form is sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party service that emails it to customerservice@pearfit.app. FormSubmit handles your submission under its own terms and privacy policy. We use what you send to reply and to talk about a pilot, and we keep it for as long as we need it for that and as a record of the conversation. Please do not put anything sensitive in the form. If you email us instead, we receive your address and your message.

The live demo and the shopper app

The demo and the app work out a size by sending the shirts you entered (a name you gave each, how it fits you, its measurements and its type) and the product you chose to our size service. The service returns an answer and does not store what you sent. In the app, your wardrobe and your planned list are kept only in your browser’s local storage on your device. You can delete them with “Reset wardrobe data” on the Account screen or by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

If the app, the size service or the scraper hits an error, a report is sent to Sentry. It holds technical details such as the error, your browser and device type and the page address, and, as with any service you connect to, your IP address is visible to it. We do not add your measurements to these reports. The app does not record sessions. We have a legitimate interest in finding and fixing faults.

The scraper page

The scraper page at /scraper is a tool for reading store product pages. The link you enter is sent to our server, which fetches that page. It sends analytics only if you accepted them on this site, because it reads the same choice. The fit numbers it saves describe products, not visitors.

Who handles data for us

  • Vercel: hosting, and Speed Insights if you accept.
  • PostHog: analytics, replays and browser error reports, if you accept.
  • Sentry: error reports from the app, the size service and the scraper.
  • FormSubmit: delivery of the inquiry form to our inbox.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

Cookies and similar storage

  • pearfit.consent.v1 in local storage remembers your analytics choice.
  • Names starting ph_, in cookies or local storage, are PostHog’s and exist only if you accepted analytics. Declining afterwards removes them.
  • pearfit.wardrobe.v1, pearfit.planned.v1 and pearfit.onboarded.v1 in local storage hold the app’s data on your device. They never leave your browser except as described above.

Your choices

Use “Privacy choices” in the footer of any page to accept or decline analytics at any time. Declining after accepting stops PostHog straight away and clears what it stored here. Vercel Speed Insights stops from the next page you open. You can also block or clear cookies and local storage in your browser.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email customerservice@pearfit.app and tell us what you would like. We will answer within the time the law requires.

Children

The site is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what we collect from you, we will say so on the site.
