What the site is

Pearfit gives information about how clothes fit. The fit guides are built from reviews and size charts that Pearfit read on store product pages, and the demo and the app suggest a size by comparing a garment’s measurements with shirts you already own. These are aids for your own decision. Fit differs between people, products and production runs, so a guide or a suggested size is not a guarantee that a garment will fit you.
